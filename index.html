Research project lead · 2025–present

Learner modelling and adaptive support in SQL-Tutor

I analyze traces of learners’ interactions with SQL-Tutor to study self-regulated learning, including how learners repair errors and make progress. The goal is to translate these patterns into better adaptive support.

Curriculum research · 2026–present

Co-designing a career-focused AI literacy curriculum

I work with middle school career and technical education teachers to design AI literacy and ethics lessons around workplace scenarios. The project examines how teachers’ expertise shapes the curriculum and how co-design supports their own learning.

    Original title and English abstract

    中小学人工智能教学平台研究与设计——以“智慧狗”人工智能教学辅助系统为例

    Research and implementation in AI education in China were still in their early stages, with fragmented instructional content, a lack of common curriculum standards, limited hands-on practice, and a shortage of teachers. Drawing on constructivist learning theory and guided discovery, the authors developed “Wise Dog,” a system for teaching AI to secondary school students. It combines short introductory lessons, a module for training convolutional neural networks, and a computer vision module for testing those models in practice. Two learning modes and dozens of training options support learners with different levels of prior AI knowledge.
